1.1 Introduction

Laurelite Importation Hub is a full-stack e-commerce platform I conceptualised, designed and developed to provide customers with a convenient and accessible online shopping experience.

The platform brings together product discovery, shopping cart management, secure checkout, online payments, customer wallets, order management and administrative operations within one application.

I built the application with a focus on usability, responsiveness, functionality and the flexibility to support future business expansion.

The platform is deployed at https://lovedigitals.online.

Figure 1.1: Laurelite Importation Hub — Production Homepage.

1.2 Project Background

My motivation for building Laurelite Importation Hub came from wanting to create something beyond the typical e-commerce website.

I noticed that many online shopping platforms followed similar designs and experiences. I wanted to build a platform with its own identity, a clean interface and features that make online shopping easier for customers.

I drew inspiration from established e-commerce platforms while developing my own approach to the user experience and application architecture.

The project also gave me an opportunity to apply my knowledge of frontend development, backend engineering, database management, cloud infrastructure and DevOps in one practical application.

1.3 Project Vision

My vision is to develop Laurelite Importation Hub into a comprehensive shopping platform that makes purchasing products easier for Nigerians.

Beyond conventional retail, the broader business vision includes creating access to products sourced from China through Nigerian preorder agents already operating there, with the goal of making products accessible at competitive prices.

1.4 Project Objectives

The major objectives of the project include:

* Building a responsive and user-friendly e-commerce platform.
* Providing customers with a convenient product discovery and shopping experience.
* Implementing secure user authentication and account management.
* Integrating reliable online payment processing.
* Developing a customer wallet and transaction management system.
* Supporting order management, cancellations and refunds.
* Providing administrative tools for managing the platform.
* Deploying the application on AWS cloud infrastructure.
* Creating a foundation that can support future marketplace expansion.

1.5 Business Model

The business concept is built around three major areas:

1. Direct Retail

Laurelite can offer products directly to customers through its own online storefront.

2. Multi-Vendor Marketplace

The long-term platform direction includes allowing independent sellers to list and manage products. The seller functionality is still an area for further development.

3. China Sourcing and Preorders

The broader business vision includes helping Nigerian customers access products sourced from China through Nigerian preorder agents operating there.

These areas represent the intended business direction, not a claim that every marketplace or preorder workflow is already operational.

1.6 Target Users

The platform is designed with the following user groups in mind:

* Customers: People who want to discover products, shop online, make payments and manage their orders.
* Administrators: People responsible for managing customers, products, orders, refunds and platform operations.
* Future marketplace sellers: Independent vendors who may eventually manage their products and sales through the platform.

1.7 Project Scope

The developed application includes:

* Customer authentication and account management.
* Product browsing and search.
* Shopping cart and wishlist.
* Checkout and Paystack payment integration.
* Customer wallet and transaction history.
* Order management and tracking.
* Refund management.
* Administrative dashboard and management tools.
* Real-time communication infrastructure.
* Cloud deployment and production configuration.

Future expansion includes a fully operational seller experience and expanded China sourcing and preorder workflows.

Figure 1.4: Laurelite Shopping Cart and Checkout Entry Point.

1.8 Development Approach

I approached the project as a practical full-stack development exercise, working across the frontend, backend, database and deployment infrastructure.

Rather than treating these areas as separate projects, I worked towards connecting them into one functioning application.

The process involved designing interfaces, developing API endpoints, creating database models, integrating external services, testing payment flows, configuring AWS infrastructure and troubleshooting production issues.

Every stage contributed to my understanding of how real-world web applications are developed, deployed and maintained.

1.9 Conclusion

Laurelite Importation Hub represents more than a website. It is a practical application of my experience in full-stack development, cloud engineering and DevOps.

Building it required patience, determination, continuous learning and the willingness to solve problems at every stage.

This documentation explains the technical decisions, architecture, implementation and deployment processes behind the platform.
